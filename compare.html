Method and limitations

  • Prices: DVF official transaction records (nature "Vente", type "Appartement"), median per m² of filtered records; sales counted both as records and estimated unique sales (2026 DVF files carry no document id, so a date|price|nature|address composite approximates unique sales). Source: data.gouv.fr/datasets/demandes-de-valeurs-foncieres (Licence Ouverte 2.0).
  • Rents: "Carte des loyers" 2025 (ANCT/DHUP) - modelled commune-level listing rent predictions, EUR/m²/month, apartment file. City aggregate = observation-count-weighted mean over arrondissements (sensitivity: unweighted mean differs by <3%; e.g. Paris 32.13 weighted vs 33.09 unweighted). Source: data.gouv.fr/datasets/carte-des-loyers-indicateurs-de-loyers-dannonce-par-commune-en-2025 (Licence Ouverte 2.0). Dwelling type matches DVF (apartments), but the size mix of sold vs listed units can differ.
  • Partial-net = gross minus 5% vacancy and 5% maintenance (assumptions, not sourced). Excluded: taxe foncière, copropriété charges, financing, furnishing, letting fees.
  • Acquisition cost used in partial-net: ~7.5% (FR: DMTO 5.81% + 0.10% CSI + ~1% notary + ~0.5% débours). Varies by department (2025-2028 temporary +0.5pt option in some departments, i.e. ~8.1%) and by buyer. Sources: impots.gouv.fr/particulier/achat-dans-lancien; service-public.gouv.fr A18183.
  • Singapore row: HDB resale prices are real, but HDB has buyer eligibility rules (citizenship/PR status, family nucleus, ethnic quotas) and buyer-specific ABSD - it is not a like-for-like investable product for every buyer, and no sourced rent is wired yet, so no yield is shown.
  • Price period (2026 H1 transactions) and rent period (2025 listing indicators) differ; both are shown per row.
