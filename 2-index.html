Data sources & coverage +

Type a city. RE-agent resolves 34,000+ cities worldwide (population 15k+) and shows what public data says: building-records lookup in Hong Kong, New York City, Chicago and four Canadian cities (Vancouver, Toronto, Calgary, Edmonton; Toronto is a dated snapshot), Zillow value and rent indices for 20 US cities, official sold-price indices for 14 UK cities, and real listing routes where they exist. Every figure names its source and date. Nothing here is invented or scored.

Data limitations +
  • Hong Kong records: not listed ≠ no problems. The s.24 and s.26 datasets only cover orders issued after 1 May 2023, refreshed monthly. Older orders, other order types, and every other kind of defect are outside this snapshot. Counts are cumulative orders issued, not outstanding - check per-order status in the BD order search. Addresses are BD's published English names; confirm the address, not just the name.
  • New York City records are live from NYC Open Data's DOB Violations dataset. Dismissed violations are history, not current state; absence of records is not proof a building is clear.
  • Chicago records are live from the city's Building Violations dataset. Status (open/complied) is as published; absence of records is not proof a building is clear.
  • Vancouver records are live from the City of Vancouver property tax report (2026 roll): per-parcel assessed land and building values, zoning, year built and tax levy. Assessed values are taxable values set by BC Assessment the previous year - not sale prices or market listings. Covers the City of Vancouver itself, not the rest of Metro Vancouver (Burnaby, Surrey, Richmond are separate cities).
  • Toronto records are a bundled snapshot of the City's RentSafeTO apartment-building evaluations (fetched 2026-11-09): city inspector scores out of 100 for rental apartment buildings of 3 or more storeys and 10 or more units. Houses, condo buildings and smaller rentals are outside this dataset, so no record says nothing about condition.
  • Calgary and Edmonton records are live from each city's current assessment roll: the taxable value estimate plus the details each city publishes. Assessed values are set the previous year for tax purposes and are not sale prices. One record per parcel - individual condo units may not appear separately.
  • Market figures are Zillow Research indices, city-level, Sep 2026: ZHVI (typical value of all homes: single-family + condo) and ZORI (typical asking rent across all rentals incl. multifamily). They describe the city, not any specific home, and they cover different housing stock - so no yield or return is computed from them.
  • UK market figures are official sold prices: average price of properties actually sold and year-on-year change, from the UK House Price Index (HM Land Registry / ONS), Aug 2026, per local authority area. They describe the area, not any specific home.
  • City search covers 34,000+ cities worldwide (population 15k+) via the bundled GeoNames city database - resolved on your device, no lookup is sent anywhere. Smaller towns: search the nearest major city. Outside the bundled US/UK market cities and the records areas (Hong Kong, NYC, Chicago, Vancouver, Toronto, Calgary, Edmonton), RE-agent resolves the city and points to a listing route where one is wired (Hong Kong) - elsewhere it says so plainly instead of inventing data.
  • Listings open on external portals (Zillow, Rightmove, 28Hse). RE-agent does not verify them. No valuations, no investment scores, no recommendation to buy or avoid anything.
