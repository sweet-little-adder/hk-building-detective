03 / What this cannot tell you

  • Not listed ≠ no problems. These datasets only cover s.24 and s.26 orders issued after 1 May 2023, refreshed monthly. Older orders, other order types, and every other kind of defect are outside this snapshot.
  • Counts are cumulative orders issued, not outstanding orders. An order may already be complied with. Check the current status of each order in the Buildings Department's order search.
  • Addresses are as published by BD, in English only, usually one row per building block. Two blocks of one estate can appear separately; a name match can also be the wrong building. Confirm the address, not just the name.
  • No prices, no valuations, no investment scores. This is a records lookup, not a survey, legal advice or a recommendation to buy or avoid anything.
