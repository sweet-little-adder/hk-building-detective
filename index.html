04 / What this cannot tell you

  • Hong Kong records: not listed ≠ no problems. The s.24 and s.26 datasets only cover orders issued after 1 May 2023, refreshed monthly. Older orders, other order types, and every other kind of defect are outside this snapshot. Counts are cumulative orders issued, not outstanding - check per-order status in the BD order search. Addresses are BD's published English names; confirm the address, not just the name.
  • New York City records are live from NYC Open Data's DOB Violations dataset. Dismissed violations are history, not current state; absence of records is not proof a building is clear.
  • Chicago records are live from the city's Building Violations dataset. Status (open/complied) is as published; absence of records is not proof a building is clear.
  • Market figures are Zillow Research indices, city-level, Sep 2026: ZHVI (typical value of all homes: single-family + condo) and ZORI (typical asking rent across all rentals incl. multifamily). They describe the city, not any specific home, and they cover different housing stock - so no yield or return is computed from them.
  • Listings open on external portals (Zillow, 28Hse). RE-agent does not verify them. No valuations, no investment scores, no recommendation to buy or avoid anything.
